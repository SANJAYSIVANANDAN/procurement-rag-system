2.3 The Supplier shall send the Customer a written reminder of the renewal date and of the non-renewal deadline between one hundred and twenty (120) and one hundred and fifty (150) days before the end of the Initial Term and of each Renewal Term. # SCHEDULE 5 – SECURITY MEASURES AND CERTIFICATIONS

1. **Certifications.** The Supplier shall maintain ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification and an annual SOC 2 Type II report covering the Platform throughout the Term. If a certification lapses, the Supplier shall notify the Customer within five (5) Business Days and restore it within ninety (90) days.
2. **Access control.** Role-based access, least privilege, multi-factor authentication for all administrative access, and review of privileged accounts at least quarterly.
3. **Encryption.** AES-256 at rest; TLS 1.2 or higher in transit; encryption keys held separately from the data.
4. **Logging and monitoring.** Administrative access to Customer Data is logged and logs are retained for at least twelve (12) months.
5. **Backups.** Encrypted backups at least every hour, retained for thirty (30) days and stored in a different EEA data centre from the primary.
6. **Vulnerability management.** Critical patches applied within seven (7) days of release.
7. **Personnel.** Background checks for personnel with access to Customer Data and annual security training.
