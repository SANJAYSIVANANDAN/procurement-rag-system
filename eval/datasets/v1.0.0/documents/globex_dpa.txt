# Globex Data Processing Addendum
# 1. Parties and Interpretation
1.1 This dpa agreement is made between Globex (the "Supplier") and the customer named in the order form (the "Customer").

# 2. Limitation of Liability
2.1 The liability of each party under this Agreement shall be limited to such amount as is reasonable in all the circumstances.

# 3. Termination for Material Breach
3.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not remedied within a reasonable period.

# 4. Fees and Payment
4.1 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt, in United States dollars.

# 5. Confidentiality
5.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party for five (5) years after disclosure, except as required by law.

# 6. Data Protection
6.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law. The Supplier shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Customer.

# 7. Breach Notification
7.1 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of a personal data breach promptly.
