# Acme Data Processing Agreement
# 1. Parties and Interpretation
1.1 This dpa agreement is made between Acme (the "Supplier") and the customer named in the order form (the "Customer").

# 2. Limitation of Liability
2.1 The total aggregate liability of the Supplier arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
2.2 The limitation in the previous clause shall not apply to liability for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

# 3. Termination for Material Breach
3.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of being notified.

# 4. Fees and Payment
4.1 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt, in United States dollars.

# 5. Confidentiality
5.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party for five (5) years after disclosure, except as required by law.

# 6. Data Protection
6.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law. The Supplier shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Customer.

# 7. Breach Notification
7.1 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

# 8. Sub-processors
8.1 The Supplier shall give the Customer thirty (30) days' notice of any new sub-processor, and the Customer may object to the appointment.

# 9. Governing Law
9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

# 10. Audit Rights
10.1 The Customer may, on reasonable notice and not more than once a year, audit the Supplier's records relating to the Services.

# 11. Force Majeure
11.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, and either party may terminate if such an event continues for more than sixty (60) days.
